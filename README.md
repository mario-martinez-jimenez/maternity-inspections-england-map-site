# England maternity inspection map

**[Open the interactive map — no sign-in required](https://mario-martinez-jimenez.github.io/maternity-inspections-england-map-site/)**

This public website contains 179 CQC maternity inspection records at 166 locations in England, covering 2022–2023: 131 national programme records and 48 additional documented visits. The additional visits are not a certified census of all inspections.

Search by hospital, trust or postcode. Filter by visit year, inspection group, reason and date precision. Select a location to see dates, context, uncertainty notes, original sources and saved document-page previews. Nearby and repeated records are clustered for display.

The monthly timeline separates routine programme visits, concern-triggered visits, follow-ups, mixed cases and unclear reasons. Click a month or use the First reported month dropdown to filter the map, list and CSV export. Each dated record is counted once by `Inspection from`; a cross-month period is not counted again in later months. There are 178 dated records and one separately selectable unconfirmed month. Publication dates are excluded. Hatching flags periods and single days with existing source qualifications, excluding address-only warnings. The flag does not necessarily mean that the month is uncertain; read the case Notes. Date flags also appear in the list and record panel. Original inspection records and Excel dates are unchanged.

Selected records also show **historical CQC maternity ratings** (overall, safe and well-led), linked to the original report pages. Retained ratings, inspections without a new rating and unverified values are distinguished. The combined April 2022 Gloucestershire report is not used to invent separate Cheltenham/Stroud ratings; South Tyneside's well-led value remains unverified. St Mary's Birth Centre uses matching publication-dated rows from the archived official CQC ratings dataset, while its PDF-preview gap remains flagged.

The **Maternity activity** section offers NHS HES provider statistics for April–March financial years 2022–23 and 2023–24: recorded deliveries, total/elective/emergency-other caesarean rates, instrumental/spontaneous birth, induction and missingness. These are trust/provider-wide figures, not hospital or birth-centre rates. Source periods, publication dates, denominators and cell references are supplied. Counts are rounded, suppressed values remain unavailable and zero known denominators do not produce 0% rates. Delivery-method rates exclude unknown method; induction uses known onset including caesarean onset and differs from NMPA's definition. Unknown shares use all reported deliveries. Rates are unadjusted for case mix and are not quality rankings or inspection-effect estimates.

Organisation changes are flagged: Yeovil uses its pre-merger provider in 2022–23 and Somerset in 2023–24; Ormskirk uses Southport/Ormskirk in 2022–23 and Mersey/West Lancashire in 2023–24. Those are not stable hospital series, and the separate 2023–24 RVY row is not silently combined. Missing provider rows for Macclesfield (2022–23) and Torbay (2023–24) remain unavailable. `clinical.js` retains the source values, identifiers, cells, file hashes and rating provenance. The Excel adds colour-coded overall maternity ratings and a provider-year indicators tab with the NHS source sheet and row for each record.

Visit dates are distinct from publication dates. There are 112 single preferred days, 66 date periods and one unconfirmed maternity follow-up date at Birmingham Heartlands. Period bounds do not establish attendance on every intervening day. St Mary's Birth Centre has no retained original PDF preview; its source links and archived CQC context are supplied and the gap is flagged.

The inspection reasons comprise 128 routine programme records, 27 concern-triggered visits, 19 follow-ups, 3 mixed cases and 2 unclear reasons. These are documented selection reasons, not evidence of exogenous timing. The map does not contain patient-level data or implement HES linkage. England is the scope because [HES describes care in English hospitals](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/data-tools-and-services/data-services/hospital-episode-statistics).

Map points use postcode coordinates checked on 6 November 2026; maternity-building or entrance locations and historical addresses are not established by geocoding. Read each record's qualifications before using it for exposure assignment.

The repository contains the website files, map data, 519 source-page PNGs and the two research downloads, published with the owner's explicit approval. Research working documents are maintained separately. Updates to `main` are deployed through GitHub Pages. The app is buildless: open `index.html` directly or serve the folder with a static HTTP server. Street tiles and external source links require internet access.
